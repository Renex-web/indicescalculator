Formula

Risk Amount = Account Size × Risk %
Point Value = Contract Value × FX Rate
Loss per 1 Lot = Stop Points × Point Value
Raw Lots = Risk Amount ÷ Loss per 1 Lot
Trade Lots = round DOWN to broker lot step
Educational/risk-management calculator only. It does not account for spread, slippage, commissions, financing, gaps, broker-specific tick rules, or guaranteed-stop charges.
